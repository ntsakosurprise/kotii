# Navigating the Job Market Post-Corona Pandemic

With so many people affected by the impact of the coronavirus, the unemployment rate has risen in recent months due to some companies laying off staff and others completely shutting down.  

This rise in unemployment implies that a significant number of people will be out looking for jobs, which in turn means there will be an increase in the total number of applicants competing for open positions.

If you are currently in the job market—or planning to start looking post-corona pandemic—we’ve outlined some strategies that will help you navigate the job market and land your dream job.

---

## Recommendations

### 1. Sharpen Your Skills
To effectively compete against your peers and land your dream job, you must stand out and remain unique among others applying for the same role. One way to stay ahead of the competition is by having something extra. **Honing your skills** will help you in that regard.

### 2. Research Companies in Your Industry
Do you have a specific company you wish to work for? Take it a step further and research them.  
Find out about their **vision and goals**, and assess how your skills can help them achieve those goals. You can then tailor or adjust your expertise to better align with what they’re seeking.

### 3. Improve Your Portfolio
Employers want candidates who bring value. The best way to demonstrate this is by **showcasing your work**.  

- Start a hobby project.  
- Volunteer to help someone in your field.  
- Document and share your work.  

No matter your industry, find something that keeps you active and proves your capabilities to potential employers.
