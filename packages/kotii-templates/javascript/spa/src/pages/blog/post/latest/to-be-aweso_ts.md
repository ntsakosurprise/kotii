# Ku Phila Siku ra Wena ro Sungula Entirhweni Lowuntshwa

I ntirho lowuntshwa, ndhawu leyintshwa, naswona swi nga endleka u hlangana ni vanhu vo tala lava u nga va tiviki. U le ka xiyimo xa munhu lontshwa exikarhi ka vanhu lava se va toloveleke. Va tiva un’wana na un’wana, va fana ni ndyangu, naswona va tirhisana swinene. Hambileswi ku nga vaka na ku hambana ka mianakanyo, va ha kuma ndlela yo tirhisana hikuva va se va tolovelelane.

Ka tlhelo lerin’wana, hi wena loyi u nga lontshwa—munhu loyi a nga tivekiki. Swi nga endleka swilo swo tala swi ta ku humelela (leswi u nga riki na matimba yo swi lawula). Ku nga va na ku vengana, mona, ni ku avanyisa loku nga twisisekiki (leswi nga hetelelaka swi tisa ku nyenya). A swi na mhaka leswaku u mani—leswi i xiyimo lexi vanhu vo tala va langutanaka na xona evuton’wini loko hi ri karhi hi famba endleleni ya vutomi.

Hambileswi u nga langutanaka na swilo swo tala hi siku ra wena ro sungula entirhweni, ku na tindlela to hunguta swiphiqo leswi nga vaka kona. Leswi landzelaka i swiringanyeto swo ku pfuna leswaku u sungula kahle.

---

## 1. Xeweta Vatirhi-kuloni
Tanihi munhu lontshwa, swi antswa leswaku hi wena u sungulaka ku xeweta van’wana ematshan’weni yo rindza. Leswi swi ku nyika nkarhi wo vulavula na vona hi nkarhi wun’we, swi hlayisa nkarhi, naswona swi susa ntshikilelo lowu nga lavekiki kumbe ku tika ka ku sungula mabulo.

## 2. Tshika Ku Tinyungubyisa
Hambileswi u nga ehleketa leswaku u tlakukile ku tlula van’wana (leswi swi nga riki ntiyiso nkarhi wo tala), kumbe u tivona onge u hlawulekile ngopfu, van’wana va nga ha ku teki hi ndlela yoleyo. Tshika ku tinyungubyisa, u hlangana na vanhu hinkwavo hi ku ringana. Ku va ni mahanyelo lamanene swi ya kule swinene.

## 3. Hlamusela Xitori lexi Hlekisaka (Loko Swi Koteka)
Un’wana na un’wana a nga kota ku hlekisa—swi lava ku tinyiketela ntsena. Loko u vona leswaku u nga olovisa moya, a wu chavi ku byela xitori lexi hlekisaka lexi fambelanaka ni xiyimo. Ku hleka swin’we swi aka vuxaka naswona swi tlhela swi kurisa ku titshemba.

## 4. U Nga Vulavuli Hi Wena
Loko u nga si vutisiwa hi ku kongoma, papalata ku vulavula ngopfu hi wena—handle ka loko ku ri hi ku hlekisa. Vanhu vo tala a va na mhaka ngopfu na leswi u swi endleke kumbe leswi u hlanganeke na swona hi siku ro sungula.

## 5. Endla Ntirho Wa Wena
Swi nga twala onge swi le rivaleni, kambe swi nkoka swinene. Endla ntirho lowu u nyikeriwaka hi lava ku kongomisaka, naswona landzela swileriso hambiloko swi nga twali kahle. Kombisa ku lehisa mbilu, naswona loko se u tolovela, nkarhi wa wena wo vonakala wu ta fika. Phela, i siku ra wena ro sungula ntsena.

## 1. Greet Your Colleagues
As a new employee, it's often better to initiate the greeting instead of waiting on others. Doing so will give you an opportunity to address them as a group, save you time, and remove unnecessary pressure—or awkward interactions.

## 2. Pocket Your Ego
Even if you believe you are above everyone (which is rarely true), or see yourself as a superhuman, you may not be as important to those people. Drop the ego and meet everyone at their level. Civility goes a long way.

## 3. Tell a Funny Story (If You Can)
Anyone can be humorous—it just takes willingness. If you feel you can lighten the mood, go ahead and share a relatable joke. A good laugh can build a sense of connection and even confidence.

## 4. Don’t Talk About Yourself
Unless you’ve been explicitly asked, avoid talking about yourself—except when joking. Most people don’t want to hear about your achievements or misfortunes on day one.

## 5. Do Your Work
This may sound obvious, but it’s very important. Carry out the tasks your seniors assign you and follow instructions, even if they come across as arrogant. Show tolerance, and once you’ve settled in, you’ll earn your time to shine. After all, it’s only your first day.
