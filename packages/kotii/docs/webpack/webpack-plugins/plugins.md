Webpack plugins are kotiijs' plugins that subscribe to webpack bundler's transpilation events. This helps us achieve our logical goals to the best of our desire. Manipulation of webpack's resources gives more control over what happens with transpile assests. Kotii has a list of plugins it uses on webpack.
